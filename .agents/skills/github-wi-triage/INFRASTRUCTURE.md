## Step 1 – Understand the Change

Determine:

- What infrastructure is changing?
- Why is the change required?
- What systems are affected?
- What dependencies exist?
- What operational problem is being solved?

---

## Step 2 – Assess Operational Impact

Document:

- Downtime expectations
- User impact
- Service impact
- Dependencies
- Operational risks

---

## Step 3 – Produce an Implementation Plan

Include:

- Systems affected
- Configuration changes
- Infrastructure changes
- Deployment steps
- Rollback strategy
- Monitoring changes
- Alerting changes
- Validation steps
- Disaster recovery considerations

---

## Step 4 – Adversarial Review

Spawn an adversarial reviewer.

Challenge the proposal by looking for:

- Single points of failure
- Missing rollback procedures
- Deployment risks
- Monitoring gaps
- Capacity concerns
- Security implications
- Hidden dependencies
- Operational complexity

---

## Step 5 – Refine

Update the infrastructure plan based on the review.

---

## Step 6 – Publish

Post the implementation plan as a comment on the GitHub issue.

The comment should contain:

1. Summary
2. Systems affected
3. Deployment plan
4. Rollback plan
5. Monitoring strategy
6. Risks
7. Outstanding questions
